PakPorter

Your wish.Their journey.Delivered.

Authentic products from abroad, carried home by verified Travellers, with your payment protected until delivery.

Try the live demo See how it works

Why it exists

Genuine international products are hard to find in Pakistan, and when they are available they are often resold at a large markup.

People fall back on relatives or social media groups, which offer no identity checks, no secure payment and no way to settle a dispute. Buyers overpay or get scammed, and Travellers can end up carrying items they should not.

How it works

  1. 1

    Make a wish

    A Buyer posts the product they want from abroad, with photos, quantity and a deadline.

  2. 2

    Travellers bid

    Verified Travellers flying a matching route offer to carry it for a fee. Each Traveller places one bid.

  3. 3

    Payment is held in escrow

    The Buyer picks a bid and the payment is held in PakPorter Points until the product arrives.

  4. 4

    Delivered and released

    The Buyer confirms with a handover code and the payment is released to the Traveller.

Built for trust

  • Route-matched TravellersBuyers only see offers from people flying their route.
  • Escrow-protected paymentsMoney moves only after delivery is confirmed.
  • Verified identity and tripsTravellers confirm who they are and where they are flying.
  • Safety screeningWishes for banned and restricted items are held for review.
  • Price guidanceA suggested range helps both sides agree a fair fee.
  • Trust scores and disputesRatings build reputation, and an admin resolves disagreements.

Where PakPorter stands

  • An interactive prototype with simulated data shows the full Buyer and Traveller journey.
  • The complete platform is under development as a final year project.
  • AI-supported features, such as safety screening and price guidance, appear in the prototype as demonstrations.
  • The planned revenue model is a tiered commission on completed deliveries. There are no live users or revenue yet, and we welcome feedback and partnerships.
